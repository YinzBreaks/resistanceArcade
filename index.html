Where it goes

Cycle 01

Two partners this cycle: one local, one national. Numbers update at each monthly reconciliation, not in real time — a tip made today appears above once it has settled and cleared.

WCFB
Local — Western PA

Westmoreland County Food Bank

The central hunger-relief hub for Westmoreland County, distributing food through a network of local pantries and drop-off sites. A member of Feeding America and Hunger-Free Pennsylvania, it uses bulk purchasing power to stretch a donated dollar far past what a retail grocery run could do.

Why this one These are my neighbors, and some of them are my students. Hunger in a county like this is quiet and constant — it never makes the news, and a kid who hasn't eaten can't learn. Storm damage just turns an ordinary bad month into an impossible one.

501(c)(3) · EIN 25‑1422682 · westmorelandfoodbank.org

WCK
National & global

World Central Kitchen

A disaster-response organization that stands up field kitchens and serves hot meals in the immediate aftermath of hurricanes, fires, floods, and conflict — usually working alongside local restaurants and cooks who are already on the ground.

Why this one Because speed is the whole point. When a disaster hits, WCK is generally cooking before the formal aid pipeline finishes mobilizing, and a hot meal on day two is worth more than a pallet of supplies on day ten.

501(c)(3) · wck.org

Partners rotate. This pair is Cycle 01. The goal is to reach as many lives as possible, so beneficiaries change as needs change — with a standing focus on disaster-affected areas, people detained by ICE and the families they leave behind, and anyone else in genuine need. Closed cycles stay in the public ledger permanently, so nothing disappears when the marquee changes.

About & mission

Who built this

I'm a teacher, a father, and a creator. I work every day, and I still struggle.

I know a lot of people are in exactly the same place — doing everything right and still coming up short at the end of the month. That's why I built the Resistance Arcade: so I can help my own family while helping as many other people as I possibly can. Both at once, out in the open, with the math published.

The mission

Free, open-source satirical games that anyone can play, on any device, with no paywalls, no corporate trackers, and no advertising. No account, no email capture, no analytics pixel following you around. Every cabinet loads and runs for free, forever. Tipping is optional and buys you nothing the next player doesn't already have.

Where the money goes

50% of net proceeds, after Stripe fees, goes to relief. Cycle 01 splits it between one local organization and one national one: 25% to the Westmoreland County Food Bank, feeding local school kids and restocking pantries for families hit by storm damage; 25% to World Central Kitchen, putting hot meals on the ground during disasters. The remaining 50% covers hosting, the domain, and building the next cabinet.

Every transfer is logged in the public ledger, reconciled on the 7th of each month, with a receipt from each organization posted as it clears.

Partners will change

The goal is to reach as many lives as I can, so the organizations rotate as needs shift. The standing focus is disaster-affected areas, people detained by ICE and the families they leave behind, and anyone else in real need. If you know a group doing that work, nominate them — the only requirement is that they clear the same bar as the current partners. Game ideas are just as welcome.

Offline preservation

Tipping for an offline edition gets you a standalone, DRM-free copy of that cabinet — the whole game in a folder you own, playable with no internet connection, no launcher, and nothing phoning home. It keeps working if this site ever goes dark, and the tip funds the same relief split as any other contribution.

Thank you

A heartfelt thank you to anyone who took the time to read this. An even bigger one to anyone who chooses to give. You are the entire reason this works. — Mike, Western Pennsylvania
